Optional local-file analysis

First choose a playback file below, then explicitly analyze it. Look-ahead supports mono/stereo PCM16 or float32 WAV (32 MiB, 10 minutes), plus locally hosted MP3, FLAC and Ogg Vorbis decoders in a terminable worker (32 MiB encoded / 3 minutes decoded / 45-second time budget / 16 kHz mono/stereo output). Stop terminates that worker, including in-flight WASM work; no native decode is started for those formats. Other browser-playable codecs such as AAC/M4A, Opus or WebM require the separate native-fallback consent below. Native decode cannot be interrupted in flight and can allocate extra memory; Stop discards its result, but native work can finish in the background. Encoded files are held whole; worker PCM is processed in chunks with bounded output, not constant total memory. Playback and tap tempo remain available if look-ahead is unsupported. A cancellable worker computes two-resolution spectra, RMS, centroid, rolloff, flatness, chroma, novelty and conservative tempo candidates. No audio is uploaded or stored.

Model/runtime files load only when requested; no audio upload or persisted predictions. Best on a single pitched instrument; percussion, mixes and unusual timbres can be wrong. These are raw note activations, not calibrated certainty or key/chord/mood/section understanding.
