World interval includes arrival. Transitions are capped at 80% of that interval, leaving time to inhabit the world. Changes apply to the next transition; interval changes apply immediately. Linger stays indefinitely. A frozen world also freezes its transition.

Lineage nursery · breed and keep offspring

Choose 2–4 different compatible parents. Only Collection VIII nutrient-agent worlds are eligible; shaders, chemistry and nonlinear attractors remain separate solver families. Authored parents are explicit eco-v1 adapters: seeded geometry, material and behavioural recipes inspired by Collection VIII, not exact copies of their original renderers. Children recombine numeric traits and whole geometry/force modules, not pixels or shader strings.

Twins share a genome but have different population/habitat seeds. Audition, name and save either; saved children can become parents. The nursery stays separate from the 320-world catalogue. Auditions turn on Linger; arrows return to authored travel.

Music laboratory · causal routes & local look-ahead

Four editable routes. Negative strength inverts a route; unchecked bypasses it. Zero global audio reactivity bypasses all routes. Trait changes are bounded to ±35% of manual values; calm/reduced-motion attenuates them further. No automatic brightness flashes.

Profiles persist only with “Remember settings” below (up to 64). Other worlds inherit global routes. Reset preferences clears all profiles. Microphone remains real-time; the analysis below never captures it.

Optional local-file analysis

First choose a playback file below, then explicitly analyze it. This initial look-ahead engine supports uncompressed mono/stereo PCM16 or float32 WAV only (32 MiB, 10 minutes). MP3 and other playable formats retain real-time response and manual tap tempo. A cancellable worker computes two-resolution spectra, RMS, centroid, rolloff, flatness, chroma, novelty and conservative tempo candidates. No audio is uploaded or stored.
