## Delivered in Collection VIII

320 authored worlds: all 256 ancestors plus 64 new constructions. Thirty-two
new shader fields and thirty-two drawn systems use different spatial and drawing
grammars. Drawn ecologies retain local state, compete over a finite nutrient
field, reproduce with two-parent gene combinations, mutate within limits, age
and renew. Some specialised worlds instead expose reaction–diffusion, nonlinear
attractor paths or persistent pigment memory. No claim of sentience, biological
realism, optimal beauty or trained intelligence is made.

New controls cover growth/reproduction, variation, ecological coupling, lifecycle
pace, atmosphere, order/asymmetry, camera drift and light persistence. Their
meaning depends on the construction: a reaction–diffusion tidepool is not a
flowering plant. Existing worlds keep their own algorithms. The shader systems
morph fields; they do not run the same individual-organism simulation as Canvas.
Higher lifecycle pace speeds both agent aging and shader gene epochs; the latter
span 8–18 simulated seconds (12.5 by default), layered with their own continuous
rhythms rather than screen-clearing resets.
The panel identifies the mechanism and, for the agent ecologies, shows live
population, birth/renewal and generation counters.

24 image-space transitions carry an outgoing snapshot into the **live incoming
world**. They are not two simultaneously simulated worlds and do not constitute
DNA breeding. World interval, transition duration, easing, style/shuffle and warp
are configurable. Reduced motion and calm replace spatial warps with a dissolve.
No additive white bursts are used. These precautions are not a photosensitivity
safety certification.

Audio now has user-tunable attack, release and sensitivity. Smooth envelopes
shape visual energy and band response, avoiding a hard loudness-to-flash mapping.
Four small, bounded routes connect bass to growth pressure, mids to coupling,
energy to atmosphere and highs to light persistence. Zero reactivity bypasses
these routes. The new music editor now makes four routes editable and bypassable,
with global fallback and per-world profiles.
This remains limited real-time analysis, not full musical understanding.

## Priority 1 — the lineage nursery

**Recommendation: a typed, bounded phenotype/genotype system, not shader-string
splicing and not a crossfade labelled as a child.** A useful child should inherit
*behaviour*, silhouette, developmental timing, interaction and material rules.

### Phase A: explicit, reproducible two-parent offspring

Define a versioned recipe independent of renderer objects:

- Stable UUID, schema version, seed, creation version and parent recipe IDs.
- Compatible habitat/solver type; geometry/motif genes; material/palette genes;
  growth, nutrient and reproduction laws; movement grammar; temporal envelopes.
- A provenance record: which parent supplied each trait, where mutation occurred,
  and whether a trait was clamped for numerical or accessibility safety.
- An immutable recipe and an optional separately saved runtime checkpoint. A seed
  alone is not a checkpoint for a history-dependent ecosystem.

Show two parents and a **small audition of children**, not an automatic promise
of one objectively best child. Offer family resemblance, balanced hybrid and
wild-child mutation. Twins can share a genotype with different seeds/habitats;
explain that distinction. Preview variations before saving. Let the user name,
like, discard, re-parent and compare siblings.

Start with compatible VIII agent/ecology families. Discrete grammar genes should
be chosen or recombined at meaningful boundaries; continuous traits can use
weighted crossover. Enforce solver bounds, finite populations, colour/flash and
motion limits. Incompatible solvers should produce an explicitly labelled layered
hybrid or be unavailable—not silently combine invalid equations.

### Phase B: a separate local nursery library

Use IndexedDB with **explicit opt-in**, capacity limits and recipe-only JSON
import/export. Keep authored catalogue and offspring library distinguishable.
Nothing is uploaded by default. Include ancestor graph, parent/sibling links,
generations, renderer/schema version and missing-parent handling. Deduplicate
recipes by canonical hash; validate imports and never execute imported code.

Saved offspring may become parents. Parent deletion needs clear options: retain
an ancestry stub, detach provenance, or delete descendants after confirmation.
Never quietly erase a user's lineage. Provide storage use, export, clear-all,
quota/error handling and safe schema migrations. Audio and file names do not
belong in lineage records.

### Phase C: guided evolution, then opt-in unattended nurseries

Use quality-diversity search rather than selecting only the most liked colour:

1. Reject unstable, blank, excessive-flash and unbounded offspring.
2. Maintain behavioural diversity bins (motion, topology, developmental rhythm,
   light density, habitat), with novelty relative to recent families.
3. Use explicit human preferences as one signal, not a universal beauty score.
4. Keep wild/calm lineages and accessibility-safe alternatives instead of one
   converged style. A chaotic child should not mean numerical instability.
5. Make generation/time/energy/storage budgets visible; allow immediate stop.

Begin with 2 parents, then 3–4 with explicit weights. A 32-parent genome should
compose *compatible modules* with sparse dominance, not average 32 complete
worlds into mud. Preserve parent contributions and let the user inspect them.
Lineages should diversify rather than produce an indiscriminate light soup.

**Current status:** 2–32-parent typed module compositions and bounded per-run
quality-diversity *proxy* search now work, with 96 candidates / 20 generations /
60 seconds / 24 survivors. The proxy is genome distance plus moderate traits and an
optional taste reference—not image analysis or proof of beauty. Real thumbnails
and human auditions remain essential. Ancestry is retained on explicit saves within
the library cap. No overnight job or runtime checkpoint is shipped.

## Priority 2 — music and world as one organism

The current browser analyzer measures energy, broad frequency bands, spectral
flux, onset density, recent dynamics and distinguishable channel balance. A tempo
estimate is shown only after consistent evidence. It does **not** infer musical
emotion, genre, key, chords, source stems, track sections or identity.

### Phase A: expressive causal mapping before larger models

- Separate transient, sustained, low-frequency and high-frequency envelopes;
  use adaptive noise floors and silence hysteresis, with bounded gain.
- Map bass to spatial pressure and slow mass, midrange to articulation/collective
  motion, highs to fine structure—not everything to brightness at once.
- Use fast attack / long release where appropriate; retain quiet microstructure
  instead of aggressive automatic gain pumping. Make each route editable and
  bypassable; provide a calm preset and per-world response profiles.
- Confidence-gated beat phase and tempo drift, with graceful free-running clocks
  when uncertain. Sync a proportion of motion, leaving other life processes free.
  Never reset all phases abruptly on a detected beat.
- Preserve musical dynamic range, contrast and rests. Avoid full-screen flashes
  on every onset. Use latency-aware smoothing and show measured delay rather
  than inventing perfect sync.

### Phase B: optional, deeper local-file analysis

Analyze **only a user-selected file**, in bounded chunks in a Web Worker/AudioWorklet:
multiresolution spectrogram, spectral centroid/rolloff/flatness, chroma, harmonic/
percussive separation, beat/downbeat candidates, novelty/self-similarity and
section-boundary candidates. Distinguish observations from musical interpretation.
Avoid calling repeated high energy a chorus or claiming that chroma proves a key.

Offer a local analysis timeline with confidence bands and corrections: tap tempo,
beat offset, halve/double tempo, add a section marker, disable an unreliable route.
No unsupported streaming-service integration or browser-tab capture is assumed.
Keep channel routing, browser autoplay/permission behaviour, Bluetooth latency and
resampling in the test plan. Mic input must never be fed back to the speakers.

A local-file pass can provide look-ahead for a long musical arc. Keep real-time mic
mode causal and label its limitations. Large/long files need memory and duration
limits, cancellation, progress and fallback to streaming analysis. Do not keep a
whole long track decoded merely to compute a thumbnail.

### Phase C: optional on-device learned features

**Initial delivery:** Basic Pitch note-activity estimates now run locally on a bounded
four-second excerpt. No chord/mood/section claim; broader phases below remain goals.

Only after evaluating permissively licensed models and realistic device budgets:
optional source separation or music embeddings, explicit model/download consent,
transparent uncertainty, no credential requests and no audio upload by default.
A model is not proof of emotional or structural truth. Avoid a mandatory large
model gate at entry. Allow complete removal of downloaded models and cached data.

### Acceptance and listening studies

Use synthetic sweeps, impulses, steady tones, asymmetric stereo, mono, silence,
variable tempo and low-SNR material; verify confidence and teardown. Add manually
annotated, legally usable music across ambient, acoustic, polyrhythmic, orchestral,
electronic and quiet material. Compare expressive mapping with a simple loudness
baseline via human preference studies; analyse failure cases rather than declaring
universal beauty. Check both headphones and muted visuals, narrow phones and
slower devices. Publish benchmark conditions; software-GPU tests do not establish
hardware FPS or low audio latency.

## Practical order

1. Review the first nursery phenotypes and music routes visually and by listening.
2. Expand compatible modules without losing per-trait provenance or finite budgets.
3. Add runtime checkpoints and richer ancestry navigation after recipe stability.
4. Broaden codecs and improve musical timing/features beyond the initial WAV worker.
5. Evaluate the delivered bounded 32-parent/module search with human review before
   considering more contributors, image-based quality metrics or longer jobs.

This is an ambitious development programme, not a promise that an assistant keeps
working unattended for six to eight hours after a session ends. Long-running work
needs an actual running job, checkpoints, logs, cancellation and a resource budget.
